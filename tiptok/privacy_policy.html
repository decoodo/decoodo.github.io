Privacy Policy

How TipTok Budget & Expenses handles information while keeping your financial records local-first and private by default.

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TipTok Budget & Expenses mobile application (“TipTok” or the “Application”) handles information. TipTok is designed to work primarily on your device. The developer does not operate a backend server that stores your budgets, transactions, or Google Sheets content.

Information stored on your device

TipTok lets you create and manage financial records such as accounts, income, expenses, transfers, budgets, bills, subscriptions, savings goals, debts, expected income, categories, tags, notes, and app settings. This information is stored locally on your device until you delete it, clear the Application’s data, perform a factory reset inside TipTok, or uninstall the Application.

Optional Google Account and Google Sheets connection

TipTok can optionally create, open, read, and update a TipTok workbook in your own Google Account. Google sign-in is not required to use TipTok’s local budgeting features.

When you connect Google, the Application may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile photo to show the connected account. TipTok uses Google authentication through the platform integration and does not store your Google password. The workbook ID and synchronization state are stored locally so the Application can update the workbook you selected.

Google API scopes

TipTok requests the following Google API scopes only when you use the optional workbook connection:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — to create and access files created by TipTok or explicitly opened for TipTok.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — to read and write the selected TipTok workbook.

TipTok does not request broad Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, location, camera, or microphone access. TipTok’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the connection and workbook features requested by the user.

Advertising and analytics

TipTok 3.0.0 is ad-free. It does not include an advertising SDK, does not request the Android Advertising ID permission, does not sell financial data, and does not use financial records for advertising or marketing. The Application does not include a developer analytics SDK.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, TipTok schedules local notifications on your device. Financial amounts are hidden from notification text by default. TipTok does not use a developer server to send push notifications.

Export, backup, restore, and sharing

TipTok can export transaction files and complete backups and can restore files selected by the user. Exported files are saved or shared only when the user chooses an Android destination or sharing app. Any recipient or third-party app selected by the user will receive the chosen file and will handle it under its own policy.

Third-party services

Optional Google Sign-In, Google Drive API, and Google Sheets API features are provided by Google and are governed by Google’s terms and privacy policy. TipTok does not include advertising or developer analytics services.

Data retention and deletion

  • Local records: Records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the Application’s data, perform a factory reset inside TipTok, or uninstall the Application.
  • Google workbook: Optional synchronized records remain in your Google Drive or Google Sheets until you delete the workbook or its contents.
  • Disconnected accounts: Disconnecting Google stops future API access but does not automatically delete a workbook from your Google Account.
  • Exported files: Files remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete those copies.
  • Developer servers: The developer does not retain your financial records or Google Sheets content on a developer server.

You can disconnect Google inside TipTok, revoke access from Google Account security settings, delete the workbook in Google Drive, delete local records in TipTok Settings, clear Android app data, uninstall the Application, and delete exported files from their chosen storage locations.

Location and children

TipTok does not collect precise location. The Application is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

TipTok uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to a local-first application. Google API communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should protect their device, Google Account, and exported backup files.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Application or legal requirements change. The effective date on this page will be updated when changes are published.
